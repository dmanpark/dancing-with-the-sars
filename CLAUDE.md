# AI Use Disclosure

The team used an AI coding assistant to accelerate development. Its role was explicitly limited to:
- **Boilerplate and UI scaffolding:** Drafting the MapLibre/HTML layout, the slider logic, and Web Audio sonification stubs.
- **Debugging and checking:** Writing test harnesses, verification scripts, and plotting utilities to help us trace errors in the GDAL/rasterio pipeline.
- **Documentation:** Formatting README sections and drafting generic docstrings.

**Team-Authored Core:** The team designed and authored all scientific logic. This includes the Kittler-Illingworth thresholding, the bimodality gate, the HH/HV flood rules, the valid-pixel mask, and the optical-reference validation (F1 metrics). The AI did not design the analysis or make the final validation calls. 

The two diagrams in the app are SVG shapes marked "Diagram made with AI assistance." No generative image, video, or audio tools were used.
